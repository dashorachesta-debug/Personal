About Me

I like products that make sense to the people using them.

My work combines product thinking, on-ground user observation and cross-functional execution with a growing focus on design-led digital experiences.

At Axis Bank's Digital Transformation vertical, I am part of the team building a sales application used by branch employees across credit cards, lending, CRM and nudge engines.

My approach goes beyond delivery. When requirements come in, I explore the complete product flow, challenge assumptions and refine experiences where the journey does not work well for the end user.

During branch visits, I observe how employees interact with the application first-hand, identify friction points and understand usage patterns that may not surface through requirement documents.

I work closely with engineering and QA teams to align technical feasibility, review builds, support testing and ensure what ultimately ships reflects the intended user experience.
